The problem

The hidden crisis of senior falls in India.

1 in 3 senior citizens in India suffers a fall every single year — many of them living alone, with no one nearby to respond. When it happens, families face an average delay of 2–4 hours before emergency help arrives.

Devices built to solve this already exist, but they cost an exorbitant ₹8,000 to ₹10,000 and are far too complicated for middle-class Indian families to adopt at scale.

Our mission is simple yet vital: to deliver instant, affordable emergency response support for elderly citizens, so every senior gets immediate assistance in critical life-or-death moments, no matter where they are.

How it works

Event to caregiver, with no phone in between.

01

Fall detected, or SOS pressed

The wearable senses a fall automatically, or the wearer presses the SOS button directly on the device.

02

Device alerts instantly

A local buzzer sounds right away while the device processes the event and reaches out over its own direct cellular connection.

03

Caregiver notified

Emergency contacts are alerted instantly by SMS and call — standalone cellular means no smartphone is ever needed.
